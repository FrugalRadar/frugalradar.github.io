Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 10, 2026

FrugalRadar ("we", "our", or "the app") is operated by Abide Labs. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

Location Data. When you grant permission, FrugalRadar accesses your device's approximate location to show deals near you. Location data is used in real-time to query nearby deals and is not stored on our servers or shared with third parties.

Usage Data. We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (e.g., screens viewed, features used) to improve the app. This data cannot identify you personally.

Merchant Submissions. If you submit a deal through our merchant portal (Tally form), the information you provide (business name, deal details, contact email) is stored in our database to display in the app.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require an account or login.
  • We do not collect your name, email, or phone number (unless you voluntarily submit a merchant deal).
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use tracking cookies or cross-app tracking.

How We Use Information

  • To display deals and events near your current location.
  • To improve app performance and user experience.
  • To display merchant-submitted deals in the app feed.

Third-Party Services

FrugalRadar uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — database and backend infrastructure. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Expo / EAS — app build and distribution. Expo Privacy Policy
  • Eventbrite — local event data (public event listings only). Eventbrite Privacy Policy

Data Retention

Location data is processed in real-time and not stored. Deal and business data is retained as long as the deal is active. Merchant-submitted information is retained until the deal expires or the merchant requests removal.

Children's Privacy

FrugalRadar is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your Choices

  • You can deny location permission at any time through your device settings. The app will still function but cannot show nearby deals.
  • Merchants can request removal of their submitted deals by contacting us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
