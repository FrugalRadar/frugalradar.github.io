ReplyAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

ReplyAI ("we", "us") is operated by Abide Labs LLC. This policy explains how we handle your data.

How the App Works

When you analyze a conversation, your message content is sent to an AI service to generate reply suggestions. No data is stored, sold, or used to train models.

Data We Collect

  • Screenshots/Images: Images you upload are transmitted to OpenAI, Inc. for analysis. They are not stored by us or by OpenAI beyond the duration of the API request.
  • Text input: Text you enter is transmitted to OpenAI, Inc. for processing. It is not stored by us.
  • Usage data: We track the number of free uses locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device.

Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, or any personal identifying information. No account is required to use ReplyAI.

Third-Party Services

ReplyAI uses the OpenAI API (operated by OpenAI, Inc.) to generate reply suggestions. Your message content is processed by OpenAI subject to their Privacy Policy. OpenAI is the sole third-party recipient of your data.
