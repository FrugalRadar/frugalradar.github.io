ReplyAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 29, 2026

ReplyAI ("we", "us") is operated by Abide Labs LLC. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Data We Collect

  • Screenshots/Images: Images you upload are sent to OpenAI's API for analysis and are not stored by us.
  • Text input: Text you enter is sent to OpenAI's API and is not stored by us.
  • Usage data: We track the number of free uses locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device.

Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, or any personal identifying information. No account is required to use ReplyAI.

Third-Party Services

ReplyAI uses OpenAI's API to generate reply suggestions. Images and text you submit are processed by OpenAI subject to their Privacy Policy.
