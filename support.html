Frequently Asked Questions

How does FrugalRadar find deals near me?
FrugalRadar uses your device's location to search for active deals from local businesses within a 10-mile radius. The closer a deal is, the higher it appears in your feed.

Why do I need to grant location permission?
Location permission allows the app to show deals near your current position. Without it, the app cannot determine which deals are nearby. Your location is never stored or shared.

How can I submit a deal for my business?
Local businesses can submit deals through our merchant submission form. Submitted deals will appear in the app after a brief review.

A deal is expired or incorrect. How do I report it?
Please email us with the deal title and business name, and we'll update or remove it promptly.

Is FrugalRadar free?
Yes. FrugalRadar is completely free to use. No subscription, no hidden fees.
